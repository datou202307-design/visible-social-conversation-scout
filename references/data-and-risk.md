# Data and risk boundary

## Operating position

This Skill supports an account owner who starts a small review session and keeps the browser visible. It is not a crawler service, posting bot, stealth system, or guarantee against platform controls.

Before every run, review current platform rules and record the review date. If the planned access is not permitted, use manual links, a documented official API, or written platform permission.

## Blocked capabilities

- background bulk collection;
- parallel page reads;
- automatic likes, saves, follows, comments, publishing, edits, deletion, or messages;
- CAPTCHA or account-challenge handling;
- retrying a platform stop through another account, adapter, device, or network;
- identity, fingerprint, header, timing, cursor, or network manipulation intended to avoid detection;
- private messages, drafts, saved or liked content, creator analytics, contacts, or non-public account data;
- sensitive-topic intervention under this general-purpose Skill;
- covert product promotion, fake customer identity, invented experience, or unsupported results.

## Data minimization

Keep only the stable public content ID, canonical URL, capture time, minimized public author label, necessary public evidence excerpt, visible counters, review decision, and response version. For voice matching, additionally keep one minimized public profile description and evidence-backed observations from no more than five recent public posts by the current account.

Do not store media files, full-page dumps, follower graphs, avatars, contact fields, precise location, private identifiers, signed navigation parameters, access credentials, browser state, or unrelated comments.

The plan must set raw-evidence retention between 1 and 30 days. Final aggregate decisions may remain after raw evidence expires only when they no longer contain unnecessary personal data. Follow the operator's deletion and legal-hold process.

## Response and disclosure boundary

Page-specific wording requires verified page understanding. Claims about spoken or visual video content require subtitles, transcript, OCR, or human review.

When AI supplied the core wording, the operator checks the platform's current AI-content declaration before publishing. A product, service, link, offer, recommendation, or resource from the account requires a truthful relationship disclosure and any applicable commercial-content review.

## Incident handling

Stop the run after CAPTCHA, account challenge, rate limit, login expiry, permission prompt, platform warning, target mismatch, abnormal redirect, unexpected private data, or two consecutive failures of the same read. Preserve a minimal local incident note. Resume only after the operator resolves the condition and explicitly restarts or resumes the same bounded run.
