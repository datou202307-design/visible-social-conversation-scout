三页九条建议 · 合成示例

A · 旧邮局这条街，傍晚慢慢走

阿沅散步

  1. 西侧有树荫这条记下了，夏天散步很需要。
  2. 1.6 公里刚好，周末不想排太满。
  3. 从旧邮局走到河边，中途有能坐会儿的地方吗？

B · 走累了，就在街角喝杯咖啡

小林的周末

  1. 窗边能看街景，比打卡布景更吸引我。
  2. 周末两点后要等位，那我会把它放在散步第一站。
  3. 外带之后，附近有能坐下来的公共长椅吗？

C · 带狗走河边，这段路有阴凉

团子的散步记录

  1. 饮水点和阴凉都标出来了，这两个信息很实用。
  2. 带狗走外圈挺合适，骑行多的那段我会绕开。
  3. 饮水点是在入口左边，还是要往里走一段？

A · An evening walk by the old post office

Arin Walks

  1. The shaded west side is a useful tip for a summer walk.
  2. 1.6 km sounds about right for a slow weekend.
  3. Are there places to sit between the post office and the river?

B · A corner café for a break from walking

Lin’s Weekends

  1. A window seat facing the street would be the draw for me.
  2. With queues after two, I’d make this the first stop on the walk.
  3. Are there public benches nearby if you get a takeaway?

C · A shaded riverside route for dog walks

Mochi’s Walks

  1. Good to have both shade and a water stop marked out.
  2. The outer loop sounds useful. I’d skip the busy cycle section.
  3. Is the water point right by the entrance or further along?
